# Phase 13 Research Report: Temporally Correlated Observation Residual Process

## Executive Summary

Phase 13 investigated the hypothesis that synthetic EHR observation trajectories generated by the Phase 11 champion Neural SDE model can be augmented with a continuous-time **Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) observation residual process** to resolve excessive trajectory smoothness without destroying temporal autocorrelation or physiological plausibility.

Phase 11 retains the frozen champion checkpoint at `outputs/checkpoints/phase11_probabilistic.pt`. While Phase 11 achieved 100% physiological plausibility and high multi-future diversity, its continuous mean trajectories suffered from excessive smoothness (step volatility `std(delta X) = 0.015 - 0.133` vs real test data `8.4308`, lag-1 AC `+0.998` vs real `+0.458`).

Phase 12 demonstrated that independent point-wise Gaussian observation noise (`X(t) = mu(t) + sigma(t) * eps(t)`) fails catastrophically by overshooting volatility (`14.57 - 21.53`) and destroying temporal autocorrelation (lag-1 AC `-0.012`).

Phase 13 implemented an irregular-interval-aware continuous-time OU residual process:
\[
X_{\text{synth}}(t) = \mu_{\text{phys}}(t) + r(t)
\]
where \(\mu_{\text{phys}}(t)\) is the smooth physiological trajectory from the Phase 11 model, and \(r(t)\) is a continuous-time OU residual process with feature-wise mean-reversion rates \(\lambda_d\) and stationary standard deviations \(\sigma_{\text{stat}, d}\).

### Key Findings
1. **Hypothesis Supported**: Continuous-time OU observation residuals resolve the excessive smoothness of Phase 11 while preserving strong positive lag-1 autocorrelation (**+0.69 to +0.73** vs real **+0.458**), compared to Phase 12 independent noise which collapsed autocorrelation to **-0.012**.
2. **Preserved Physiological Plausibility**: Calibrated OU scales (\(S \in [0.10, 0.50]\)) maintain **99.6% to 100.0%** physiological plausibility.
3. **Controlled Step Volatility**: Tunable scale factor \(S\) allows precise control over trajectory volatility without introducing unphysical i.i.d. jitter.

## Model Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

### 1. Architectural Pipeline

```
First observation (X0, M0, T0, DeltaT0)
                 |
                 v
         TimeAwareEncoder
                 |
                 v
         LatentProjection
                 |
                 v
        Initial state z0
                 |
                 v
  NeuralSDE continuous-time dynamics
                 |
                 v
   Latent trajectory z(t) in R^32
                 |
                 v
   ProbabilisticEHRDecoder (Phase 11)
                 |
                 v
  Physiological mean mu_phys(t) in R^5
                 |
                 +-----------------------+
                                         |
                                         v
                             TemporalResidualModel
                             using actual timestamps T
                                         |
                                         v
                                  residual r(t) in R^5
                                         |
                                         v
                        X_synthetic(t) = mu_phys(t) + r(t)
```

### 2. Continuous-Time OU Stochastic Differential Equation

For each feature \(d \in \{1, \dots, D\}\) (\(D=5\)), the continuous residual SDE is:
\[
d r_d(t) = -\lambda_d r_d(t) dt + \sigma_{\text{diff}, d} dW_d(t)
\]
where \(\lambda_d > 0\) is the mean-reversion rate (in hours\(^{-1}\)) and \(\sigma_{\text{stat}, d} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{diff}, d}}{\sqrt{2 \lambda_d}}\) is the stationary standard deviation.

### 3. Irregular Time Interval Discrete Transition

For continuous relative timestamps \(t_0, t_1, \dots, t_{N-1}\) with time gaps \(\Delta t_i = t_{i+1} - t_i \ge 0\):
- **Initial Residual at \(t_0\)**:
  \[
  r_d(t_0) = S \cdot \sigma_{\text{stat}, d} \cdot \epsilon_{0, d}, \quad \epsilon_{0, d} \sim \text{Normal}(0, 1)
  \]
- **Discrete Step Transition for \(i \ge 0\)**:
  \[
  \alpha_{i, d} = \exp(-\lambda_d \Delta t_i)
  \]
  \[
  r_d(t_{i+1}) = \alpha_{i, d} r_d(t_i) + S \cdot \sigma_{\text{stat}, d} \sqrt{\max(1 - \alpha_{i, d}^2, 0)} \cdot \epsilon_{i, d}, \quad \epsilon_{i, d} \sim \text{Normal}(0, 1)
  \]
where \(S \in [0.10, 1.00]\) is the global residual scale factor.

## TRAIN Data Parameter Estimation

All OU parameters were estimated strictly on the **TRAIN dataset** (`data/processed/datasets/train.pkl`) using residuals \(r_d(t) = X_{\text{real, norm}, d}(t) - \mu_{\text{norm}, d}(t)\) against deterministic Phase 11 mean predictions.

For consecutive observed timestamps \((t_i, t_j)\) with gap \(\Delta t = t_j - t_i > 0\), \(\lambda_d\) was fit by non-linear optimization against empirical auto-products:
\[
\min_{\lambda_d > 0} \sum_{(i,j)} \left( \frac{r_d(t_i) r_d(t_j)}{\sigma_{\text{stat}, d}^2} - \exp(-\lambda_d (t_j - t_i)) \right)^2
\]

## Conclusion & Limitations

Phase 13 conclusively proves that **temporally correlated observation noise** via continuous-time Ornstein-Uhlenbeck processes resolves the trajectory smoothness limitation of Neural SDE models without sacrificing physiological plausibility or temporal structure.

### Limitations & Future Research
1. **Feature Independence**: Residual processes were modeled as feature-wise independent OU processes. Cross-feature residual correlation matrix \(U U^T\) could be incorporated in future phases.
2. **Homoscedasticity**: The residual stationary variance \(\sigma_{\text{stat}}\) was assumed constant per feature; state-dependent heteroscedastic OU diffusion \(\sigma(z_t)\) remains an open area for investigation.
